## Add a term

1. Open **Dictionary** in the desktop app.
2. Enter a word or name using the spelling you want.
3. Add it to your dictionary, then try it in a dictation.

TypeAside sends dictionary terms as a transcription prompt and asks the cleanup model to preserve their spelling. Recognition still depends on the provider and audio quality.

## Remove a term

Hover over the entry and choose **Remove**.

Use the dictionary for vocabulary. Use [Snippets](/docs/features/snippets) when a phrase should expand into a longer piece of text.
