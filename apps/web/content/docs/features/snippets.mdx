## Create a snippet

1. Open **Snippets** and choose **New snippet**.
2. Enter a **Trigger**, such as “my meeting link”.
3. Enter the text that should replace it.
4. Choose **Save**.

Dictate the trigger in a sentence or on its own. Hermes expands snippets after dictation cleanup. Triggers match without case sensitivity and at word boundaries. Longer triggers are checked first.

Choose a distinctive phrase that your transcription provider recognizes reliably. A phrase you say in ordinary conversation may expand when you did not intend it to.

## Change or remove a snippet

Click an existing snippet to edit it. Choose **Save** to keep changes or **Delete** to remove it.
