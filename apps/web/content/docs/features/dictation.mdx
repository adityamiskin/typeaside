## How to use it

1. Focus a text field.
2. Start recording with your dictation key. The default is `RIGHTCTRL`.
3. Speak, then stop recording.
4. Wait for TypeAside to transcribe, clean up, and paste the text.

## Hold or toggle

In **Settings → Shortcuts → Dictate**, choose **Hold** or **Toggle**. This mode also applies to the Command binding.

- **Hold**, the default: hold the key while speaking and release it to finish.
- **Toggle**: press once to start, then press again to finish.

Very short accidental taps are ignored. Press the cancel shortcut, `ESCAPE` by default, to cancel recording or pending processing.

## The overlay and sounds

The overlay shows recording and processing states. **Settings → Behavior → Sound cues** controls recording cues. **Lower media volume** reduces media volume during recording on Linux through `wpctl`. Media ducking has no effect on macOS or Windows; sound cues still play.

## Paste again

Use **Paste last**, `LEFTALT+LEFTSHIFT+V` by default, to paste your latest dictation into the currently focused app. **Press Enter after paste** is optional and off by default.

<Callout type="info">
  Leave automatic Enter off in chat apps until you are comfortable reviewing the result.
</Callout>
