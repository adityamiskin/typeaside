## Free

A Hermes account includes **2,000 hosted words per week**. The allowance resets **Monday at 00:00 UTC**. Words are counted from the transcription before cleanup, so the count can differ from the final pasted text.

When the allowance is used, later hosted transcription requests are blocked until the reset or an upgrade. A request already in progress can finish above the limit.

## Pro

Pro includes unlimited hosted words with an active subscription:

- **$12 per month** with monthly billing.
- **$96 per year** with yearly billing.

Open [Account](/account) to view usage or manage your plan. The pricing page shows the currently offered plans.

## Bring your own key

BYOK does not need a Hermes subscription or account. Your provider handles usage and billing. Configure it under [Transcription](/docs/settings/transcription).
