TypeAside turns speech into text in the app you are using. Record a thought, let TypeAside transcribe and clean it up, and paste the result into your text field.

Run transcription on-device or connect your own OpenAI-compatible provider. The desktop app also supports voice editing, personal vocabulary, and snippets.
